# 📊 Business & Sales Analytics (SQL & Excel)

## Overview
This project delivers end-to-end analysis of **sales, revenue, and profitability** using SQL and Excel.  
It combines powerful SQL queries with interactive Excel dashboards to uncover actionable insights that support business strategy and decision-making.

## Key Analysis
- **Revenue Trends**: Track annual revenue, market share by gender/category, and regional performance.  
- **Profit Margin Analysis**: Monthly, category, and sub-category breakdowns to identify high/low-margin areas.  
- **Customer Segmentation**: Age group, gender, and market share segmentation to highlight profitable segments.  
- **Regional Comparison**: Revenue and profit across countries/states to uncover strengths and weak zones.  
- **Pricing Evaluation**: Unit cost vs. unit price analysis to detect overpriced categories and low-demand products.  
- **Risk Control**: Identification of loss-making transactions, abnormal high-cost cases, and negative margin categories.  

## Deliverables
- Interactive **Excel dashboards** for visualization.  
- SQL scripts for data extraction, transformation, and analysis.  
- Documentation of methodology and insights.  

## Insights Delivered
- Highlighted profitable customer segments.  
- Identified categories with negative margins for corrective action.  
- Provided visibility into regional strengths and weaknesses.  
- Supported pricing strategy by evaluating markup and demand.
